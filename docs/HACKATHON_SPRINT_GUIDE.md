# SmritiSaathi 5-Day Hackathon Sprint Guide

**Goal**: Deliver a production-grade demonstration of SmritiSaathi with 6 contributors across 5 intensive sprint days.

---

## Day-by-Day Sprint Execution Plan

### Day 1: Architecture, Scaffolding & Setup (COMPLETED)
- Establish feature-based folder architecture and independent Route Groups.
- Build shared layouts (Patient mobile-first, Caregiver responsive, Practitioner desktop).
- Setup TypeScript domain types and UI primitives.
- Finalize team folder ownership matrix and documentation.

### Day 2: Core Domain Implementation (Parallel Sprints)
- **Contributor 1**: Memory scrapbook data models and photo reminiscing cards.
- **Contributor 2**: Daily rhythm step checklist and circadian time provider.
- **Contributor 3**: Errorless brain activities and nostalgia quiz cards.
- **Contributor 4**: Caregiver status cards and medication schedule manager.
- **Contributor 5**: Practitioner patient cohort list and clinical review modal.
- **Contributor 6**: Server-side Gemini prompt engineering and conversational mock responder.

### Day 3: Media, Audio & AI Integration
- **Contributor 1**: Family audio voice note playback and caption overlay.
- **Contributor 2**: Auditory reminder chime and voice notification hooks.
- **Contributor 3**: Audio music memory pairing and nostalgic song playback.
- **Contributor 4**: Family story upload form and memory album curation.
- **Contributor 5**: Longitudinal adherence charts using Recharts.
- **Contributor 6**: Gemini 2.5 Flash server route with emotional tone calibration.

### Day 4: Cross-Portal Synchronization & State Coordination
- Connect Caregiver updates (new memories/routines) to Patient viewports.
- Connect Patient activity completion events to Caregiver alert stream and Practitioner analytics.
- Conduct cross-browser testing on mobile devices (iOS Safari, Android Chrome).

### Day 5: Accessibility Audit, Edge Cases & Demo Recording
- WCAG AAA contrast and touch-target audit (≥56px touch areas).
- Errorless flow validation (verify zero red error states or countdown stress).
- Finalize Vercel deployment preview and record pitch video.
