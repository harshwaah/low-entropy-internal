## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a restorative, grounded, and authoritative environment tailored for neurological care specialists, geriatricians, and cognitive clinical teams. Unlike conventional medical interfaces reliant on sterile clinical whites and harsh diagnostic cyans, this design system introduces a therapeutic, biophilic-informed atmosphere. It balances the precision of an analytical decision-support tool with the human empathy essential to long-term memory care.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Biophilic Clinical Modernism:** The system pairs organic, warm paper-grade foundational surfaces with deep forest hues and botanical greens, neutralizing clinical fatigue and screen glare during prolonged review sessions.
- **Atmospheric Reassurance:** Attention is guided through controlled warmth rather than panic-inducing saturation. Visual alerts employ terracotta and amber to signal cognitive decline trajectories or biomarker deviations without inducing high-arousal visual anxiety.
- **Quiet Precision:** High-density longitudinal data, MMSE/MoCA scores, and neuroimaging metadata are nested within soft-contoured modules, generous negative space, and disciplined typography.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for prolonged diagnostic reading and high cognitive clarity. High-frequency digital blues and fluorescent accents are strictly avoided to preserve ocular comfort and visual composure.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FBF9F5`):** The primary app backdrop. A soft, warm ivory providing high legibility without the harsh contrast of pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Canvas Muted / Sub-tier (`#F5F2EB`):** Used for peripheral panels, collapsible inspector panes, and table headers.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority clinical workspace cards and active modal dialogues stand clean against the ivory background.
- **Warm Neutral Border (`#E8E4DC`):** Defines content boundaries with low visual friction.

### Primary & Diagnostic Greens
- **Primary Deep Forest (`#1E4D3A`):** The core brand color for high-emphasis actions, persistent navigation bars, and top-tier metrics.
- **Primary Deep Forest Shade (`#14382A`):** Used for hover states, key micro-interactions, and active navigation indicators.
- **Muted Sage (`#628773`):** Applied to secondary actions, secondary data series, and contextual iconography.
- **Sage Tint (`#E8EFE9`):** Wash tone for selected patient table rows, stable cognitive test indicators, and inactive badges.

### Alerts & Severity Tiers
- **Critical Deviation / Alert (`#D96B43`):** Soft warm terracotta for severe cognitive loss alerts, medication contraindications, and rapid risk triggers.
- **Critical Tint (`#FDF2ED`):** Background wash for high-severity notifications and risk chips.
- **Moderate Observation (`#D98A2B`):** Warm amber used for fluctuating biomarkers, borderline assessment tests, and pending follow-ups.
- **Moderate Tint (`#FEF7EE`):** Background wash for medium-priority warnings.

### Typography Colors
- **Deep Charcoal Green (`#1C2520`):** Primary text color for patient headers, diagnostic results, and critical metrics.
- **Muted Slate Green (`#414D46`):** Secondary text color for clinical labels, timestamps, metadata, and supporting notes.

## Typography

This design system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across display, body, and label roles. Its geometric yet humane construction features subtle curves, large x-heights, and wide apertures that ensure clinical readability across dense diagnostic tables, chart callouts, and longitudinal tracking summaries.

### Application Guidelines
- **Numeric Data:** Tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced for cognitive test scores (e.g., MoCA /30, MMSE /30), time stamps, lab ranges, and patient age displays.
- **Hierarchy Separation:** Section titles rely on weight contrast (`600` semi-bold) rather than drastic point-size increases, preventing visual dominance over medical data visualizations.
- **Label Formatting:** Category tags, vital markers, and clinical column labels utilize `label-sm` with slight uppercase tracking (`+0.04em`) to establish crisp structure in high-density areas.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a flexible multi-tier grid with an 8px base grid rhythm (supported by a 4px sub-grid for tight metric lockups).

### Grid Structure
- **Desktop (1280px and wider):** 12-column responsive fluid grid. Left-hand persistent navigation rail at a fixed 260px width. Patient summaries and active charts occupy 8 columns, while predictive triage notes and decision-support alerts occupy the right 4-column contextual rail. Margins: `2.5rem`, Gutters: `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. The navigation rail collapses into an icon-docked rail (72px). The triage inspector slides into an overlay panel. Margins: `2rem`, Gutters: `1rem`.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** Single-column stacked stream. Margins: `1rem`, Gutters: `0.75rem`.

### Visual Breathing Room
Generous whitespace surrounding data clusters prevents cognitive fatigue. Patient profile summaries and diagnostic graphs are isolated within independent structural cards spaced apart by `1.5rem` (`space-lg`) to `2rem` (`space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids dark drop shadows and floating skeuomorphic effects, prioritizing subtle ambient elevation that integrates naturally with the ivory canvas.

### Ambient Warm Shadows
Shadows incorporate a faint warm tint (`#1C2520` blended with `#1E4D3A` at minimal opacity) rather than pure neutral blacks:
- **Flat / Recessed Surface:** `0 0 0 1px #E8E4DC` (used for standard resting cards and table rows).
- **Elevation Level 1 (Resting Cards & Clinical Tiles):**
  - Border: `1px solid #E8E4DC`
  - Shadow: `0px 2px 6px rgba(28, 37, 32, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(28, 37, 32, 0.02)`
- **Elevation Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Widgets):**
  - Border: `1px solid #D8D2C6`
  - Shadow: `0px 6px 16px rgba(28, 37, 32, 0.06), 0px 2px 4px rgba(28, 37, 32, 0.03)`
- **Elevation Level 3 (Decision Support Drawers & Dialogs):**
  - Shadow: `0px 12px 32px rgba(20, 56, 42, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px rgba(28, 37, 32, 0.04)`

### Tonal Stratification
Depth is primarily reinforced tonally: the deepest tone (`#1E4D3A`) anchors fixed utility framing, content floats on crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces, and contextual workspaces rest on `#F5F2EB`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, human-centric geometry centered on `rounded-2xl` for primary containers, softening the technical clinical layout without feeling casual or imprecise.

### Radius Distribution
- **Cards, Clinical Data Containers, & Modals:** `rounded-2xl` (16px / 1rem to 24px / 1.5rem depending on viewport scale) provides an approachable form factor.
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, & Select Pickers:** `rounded-lg` (8px / 0.5rem to 10px) delivers clear affordance and structural contrast against large outer containers.
- **Badges, Filter Chips, & Status Tags:** Pill geometry (`rounded-full`) immediately separates interactive tags and medical classification labels from actionable rectangular controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1E4D3A`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `rounded-lg`, internal padding `10px 18px`. Hover: `#14382A`. Focus: `2px` offset outline in `#628773`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#E8EFE9`, text `#1E4D3A`, border `1px solid transparent`. Hover: Background `#D7E4D9`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#1E4D3A`, border `1px solid #E8E4DC`. Hover: Background `#F5F2EB`.
- **Alert / Action Required:** Background `#D96B43`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover: `#C35B34`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E4DC`, text `#1C2520`, placeholder `#7E8A83`, radius `rounded-lg`, height `42px`, padding `0 14px`. Focus state triggers border `1.5px solid #1E4D3A` with no harsh colored outer halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Inactive border `#B8B2A6`, active fill `#1E4D3A` with a crisp white checkmark/dot.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Normal / Stable Cognitive Function:** Background `#E8EFE9`, text `#1E4D3A`, dot `#628773`.
- **Attention / Moderate Decline:** Background `#FEF7EE`, text `#D98A2B`, border `1px solid #F6D6AE`.
- **High Risk / Alert:** Background `#FDF2ED`, text `#D96B43`, border `1px solid #F5C6B3`.
- **All Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `24px`, padding `2px 10px`, typography `label-sm`.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- Base card utilizes `#FFFFFF` on the `#FBF9F5` canvas, enclosed by a `1px` border in `#E8E4DC`, rounded with `rounded-2xl` (16px), and padded with `24px` (`space-lg`).
- Header areas feature clean dividers in `#F5F2EB` and group contextual patient metrics (e.g., baseline date, care partner presence) opposite action buttons.

### Specialized Cognitive Care Components
- **Cognitive Trajectory Visualizer:** Soft canvas graph component plotted on `#FBF9F5`, utilizing deep forest lines for projected baselines, muted sage for population averages, and warm terracotta markers for confirmed clinical drop-offs.
- **Clinical Decision Prompt:** A callout container structured with a `4px` left accent line (`#D98A2B` or `#D96B43`), subtle `#FDF2ED` or `#FEF7EE` background, and compact metadata rows displaying recommended pharmaceutical or therapeutic adjustments.
